ABOUT NEWSWAVE

Independent media, built from the ground up.

NewsWave is an independent journalism project created by Daniel Thiele to cover the people, events and issues shaping communities in British Columbia, Canada and beyond.

The focus is direct reporting: attend events, speak to people, ask questions, document what happened and publish clear, accessible coverage.

WHY I STARTED NEWSWAVE

I wanted to report stories myself.

I started NewsWave because I wanted a place where I could go directly to events, ask my own questions and publish what I learned. The goal is simple: show up, listen, verify what can be verified and give people a clearer picture of what is happening.
