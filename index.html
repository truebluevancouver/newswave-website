ABOUT ME

Hi, I’m Daniel.

I created NewsWave as an independent journalism project focused on the people, events and issues shaping our communities.

I cover public events, interview people directly, take photos and video, and report from the scene whenever possible.

WHY I STARTED NEWSWAVE

I wanted to report stories myself.

I started NewsWave because I wanted a place where I could go directly to events, ask my own questions and publish what I learned.

The goal is simple: show up, listen, verify what can be verified and give people a clearer picture of what is happening.
