Welcome to NewsWave.

My name is Daniel Thiele, and I created NewsWave as an independent journalism project dedicated to reporting on the stories, events, and issues that shape our communities.

Through interviews, event coverage, photography, and on-the-ground reporting, I aim to provide accurate, accessible, and engaging journalism from across Canada and beyond.

Whether I am covering a public rally, interviewing a community member, or reporting on an important issue, my goal is to help readers better understand the people and events shaping the world around them.

Thank you for visiting NewsWave.
