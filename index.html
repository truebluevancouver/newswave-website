ABOUT

About NewsWave

NewsWave is an independent journalism project created by Daniel Thiele and focused on the people, events and issues shaping our communities.

I cover public events, speak with people directly, take photos and video, and report from the scene whenever possible.

WHY NEWSWAVE

Why I started it

I wanted a place where I could go directly to events, ask my own questions and publish what I learned.

The goal is simple: show up, listen, verify what can be verified, and give people a clearer picture of what is happening.
