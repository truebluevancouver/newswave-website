ABOUT ME

Hi, I’m Daniel.

I created NewsWave as an independent journalism project focused on the stories, events and issues shaping our communities.

I cover public events, interview people directly, take photographs and video, and report from the ground whenever possible. My goal is to make reporting understandable, accessible and connected to what people are actually seeing and experiencing.

NewsWave is independent and is not part of a major media corporation.

WHY

WHY I STARTED NEWSWAVE

I wanted to report stories myself — not just watch them from a distance.

I started NewsWave because I wanted a place where I could go directly to events, speak with people, ask my own questions and publish what I learned. The goal is simple: show up, listen, verify what can be verified, and give people a clearer view of what is happening.

As NewsWave grows, I want it to become a home for original reporting, interviews and community stories that might otherwise receive little attention.
